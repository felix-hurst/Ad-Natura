=== (G.4) Functionality Overview

The following are the functional requirements for our 2D pixel platformer game in no particular order.

[[g4-sma]]
1. Slime mold will be attracted to water, repelled by light and calamity, destroyed by calamity, and react to sound by dimming and minimizing movement while high frequencies are present. Slime mold will also decompose wet wood on contact.

2. Slime mold spores will be destroyed by light, and can only turn into fully-fledged slime mold organisms in areas with low light level.

3. Destructible environment. Players destroy calamity objects or cracked stone to alter topography and release trapped water, which serves as a biological lure to redirect the slime mold.

4. Replayable and reproducible levels. The combination of stochastic slime growth and unpredictable physics ensures every playthrough offers unique spatial challenges while maintaining a logically solvable core.
